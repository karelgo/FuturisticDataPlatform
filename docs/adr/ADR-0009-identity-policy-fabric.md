# ADR-0009: One Identity Fabric — Keycloak ⊕ SPIFFE/SPIRE ⊕ OpenFGA ⊕ Generated OPA

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07

## Context

ODAP had Keycloak for humans and handwritten Rego for Trino, joined by a custom access bridge (OpenMetadata task → Keycloak realm role → OPA grant). Workloads had no identity of their own, agents had no governed identity at all, and every policy was handwritten — unportable, unauditable at scale, and drift-prone.

## Decision

One fabric, four cooperating parts, zero parallel paths:

- **Keycloak** (OIDC/OAuth) is identity for **humans and agents** — agents authenticate with their own service identities (client-ID metadata documents, incremental scopes).
- **SPIFFE/SPIRE** issues workload SVIDs (hourly rotation, mTLS everywhere) — infrastructure components authenticate as strongly as users.
- **OpenFGA** holds relationship-based access (who/what may touch which product), enforced at the catalog (Lakekeeper) before credentials are vended.
- **OPA** makes query-time row/column/purpose decisions — with **Rego generated by the contract compiler** from ODCS classification tags, never handwritten.
- **Consent and purpose ride as OIDC token claims**, evaluated by OPA at query time: the same mask applies to an analyst in a dashboard and to an agent's MCP call.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Cedar / Oso instead of OPA:** capable engines, but OPA's Trino integration and decision-log ecosystem are the mature path; the compiler could retarget Cedar later since policies are generated, not handwritten.
- **Catalog-only enforcement (no query-time layer):** cannot express row/column/purpose masking.
- **Keeping the ODAP access bridge:** the pattern (custom glue) is the thing being retired.

## Consequences

- Access self-service becomes policy-decided product behavior (portal request → purpose check → OpenFGA tuple), with the synthetic-twin fallback while approvals run.
- Because Rego is generated, policy coverage is exactly contract coverage — an unclassified column fails CI, not an audit.
- OPA decision logs and Lakekeeper CloudEvents feed the evidence plane (ADR-0010): enforcement and proof are the same pipeline.
